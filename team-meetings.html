Biweekly team meeting

Every other week the team steps out of the day to work on the practice itself: systems that are not holding, problems that keep coming back, and coordination between roles.

What this meeting is for

The morning huddle handles today. The team meeting handles the pattern behind today. If the same problem shows up in three huddles, it belongs here.

This training page is being written. Until it is published, the rules that apply to every meeting apply to this one in full.

What the finished page will cover

Planned outline, to be filled in.

  • Purpose of the biweekly team meeting and how it differs from the huddle
  • Standing agenda and how long each segment runs
  • Who attends, who leads, and who records the minutes
  • How topics get added to the agenda before the meeting
  • Preparation expected from each role
  • How action items are assigned and reviewed at the next meeting
  • What belongs in this meeting and what should go somewhere else
  • Where the minutes are stored and who reads them
